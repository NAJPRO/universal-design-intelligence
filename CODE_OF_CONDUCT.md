# Code of Conduct

UDI is a community project. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms the community. To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately when a private contact channel is available.
