# Universal Design Intelligence

UDI provides a repeatable workflow for frontend design and implementation. It is being developed in phases; use only the capabilities and catalog actually present in the installed package.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the project and identify its framework, styling approach, existing components, and design conventions.
2. Clarify the user’s goal and whether the task is a new interface or an improvement.
3. Consult installed UDI knowledge relevant to the interface and framework. Do not invent catalog results or claim a lookup happened when it did not.
4. Choose and briefly explain a design direction that fits the product and audience.
5. Reuse existing tokens and components. Do not replace an established design system without explicit permission.
6. Implement using the existing stack and established project patterns.
7. Check responsive behavior, keyboard interaction, semantic structure, and accessible naming.
8. Review the result for consistency and correct issues. Do not claim visual or accessibility validation unless the corresponding tools were run.

## Design priorities

- Prefer clarity, hierarchy, and usable interaction over novelty.
- Keep motion purposeful, restrained, and compatible with reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid generic layouts and decorative effects that weaken readability or task completion.
- Preserve existing project behavior and surface assumptions or limitations.
- Do not introduce external services, dependencies, or destructive project changes without need and authorization.
